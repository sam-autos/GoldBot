FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about GoldBot.

What does GoldBot trade?

The initial version of GoldBot is being designed specifically for XAUUSD, commonly known as gold.

What platform does GoldBot use?

GoldBot is being developed for MetaTrader 5 (MT5) using an Expert Advisor.

Does GoldBot hold my money?

No. The intended model is for users to maintain their own brokerage account and retain control of their own funds.

Can I stop GoldBot?

Yes. An Expert Advisor can be disabled through the MetaTrader platform, subject to the state of any trades that are already open.

Does GoldBot guarantee profits?

No. No trading system can guarantee a particular financial outcome. Trading involves the possibility of losses.

Can I change the risk settings?

The final EA will contain defined risk-management settings. The available settings will be explained in the installation documentation.

Should I use a demo account first?

Users should consider testing the EA in a demo environment before deciding whether to use it with real funds.

Can I lose money?

Yes. Trading XAUUSD and other leveraged financial instruments involves significant risk, including the possibility of losing trading capital.
