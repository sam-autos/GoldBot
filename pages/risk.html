RISK DISCLOSURE

Understand the Risks

Automated trading can provide systematic execution, but it does not eliminate financial market risk.

Trading involves risk

Trading leveraged financial instruments can result in losses. You should only trade with money you can afford to lose and should understand the risks before using an automated trading system.

Market volatility

Gold prices can move rapidly in response to economic data, central-bank decisions, geopolitical events, changes in interest rates and other market conditions.

Execution and slippage

Actual execution prices may differ from expected prices. Spread widening, slippage, latency and broker execution conditions can affect trading results.

Technology risk

Automated trading depends on computers, internet connectivity, MetaTrader 5, broker infrastructure and other technology. Technical failures can interfere with trade execution or monitoring.

Past performance

Past performance, backtest results or simulated results do not guarantee future performance. Actual results can differ substantially from historical or simulated results.

You remain responsible for your account

GoldBot does not remove the responsibility of monitoring your trading account. Users should understand the EA settings and monitor their account and trading environment.

Important Notice

GoldBot is an automated trading tool. It does not guarantee profits, eliminate losses or guarantee any particular trading outcome.

Before using the system with real funds, users should understand the product, the relevant broker terms and the risks associated with leveraged trading.
