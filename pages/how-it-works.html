How GoldBot Works

GoldBot is designed to automate a predefined XAUUSD trading strategy through MetaTrader 5.

01

Open your trading account

Open your own account with a supported broker.

02

Install MetaTrader 5

Install MT5 and log in using your own trading account credentials.

03

Install GoldBot

Install the Expert Advisor and attach it to the appropriate XAUUSD chart.

04

Configure your settings

Configure the available trading and risk-management parameters.

05

Monitor your account

Monitor trades, account equity and risk. You remain in control of your own account.

06

Stop whenever you choose

You can disable the Expert Advisor or close trades through your MT5 platform.

Important

Automated trading does not eliminate market risk. Trading results can vary because of market conditions, spreads, slippage, execution and other factors.
